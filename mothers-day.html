Campaign Plan

Mother's Day
Campaign Plan

A campaign that combines website gifting experience, social media engagement, and emotional storytelling to drive both traffic and conversions.

Website & SEO Social Media Combined Content 12 Tactics
Objective

Campaign Objective

The campaign supports both website traffic/conversion and social media engagement, while making the brand feel more personal, emotional, and gift-relevant during the occasion.

Goals

Campaign Goal

Increase seasonal engagement across social media

Drive traffic to the website through Mother's Day-focused content

Improve product discovery through gifting-based merchandising

Encourage conversions through campaign-led offers and curated shopping

Track 01

Website / SEO

Building a clear gifting destination on site, keeping the campaign visible, and capturing seasonal search demand.

1

Dedicated Mother's Day Landing Page

What we would do

Create a separate Mother's Day page to collect all relevant products, offers, and campaign content in one place.

How it will work

It gives users one clear shopping destination and makes campaign traffic easier to convert.

Example
Shop Mother's Day Gifts page with bestsellers, bundles, and gifting picks.
2

Mother's Day Offer Strip

What we would do

Add a top strip on the website to highlight the Mother's Day offer.

How it will work

It keeps the campaign visible across the site and creates urgency.

Example
Mother's Day Special: Flat 20% Off on Gift Picks
3

Mother's Day Gift Label on Products

What we would do

Add a small "Mother's Day Gift" tag on selected products if technically possible.

How it will work

It helps users quickly identify gifting-friendly products while browsing.

Example
Mother's Day Pick
Perfect for Gifting
4

Mother's Day Theme Blog

What we would do

Publish a blog around Mother's Day gifting, self-care, or celebration ideas.

How it will work

It supports SEO, brings seasonal traffic, and can also direct readers to products.

Example
5 Mother's Day Gifts She'll Actually Use
5

Mother's Day Buying Guide

What we would do

Launch one buying guide specially for Mother's Day.

How it will work

It helps customers choose the right gift based on need, type, or budget.

Example
Mother's Day Gift Guide: How to Choose the Right Gift for Every Mom

2

Story Engagement Content

What we would do

Use story polls, quizzes, and Q&A to involve the audience.

How it will work

It increases interaction and makes the campaign more fun and personal.

Examples
Poll: What would your mom choose?
Q&A: What's the funniest thing your mom says?
3

Reel Content

What we would do

Create relatable and product-led Mother's Day reels.

How it will work

It improves reach and gives us engaging content that people can relate to and share.

Examples
Things Moms Say in Real Life
Best Mother's Day Picks from Our Website
4

Carousel Content

What we would do

Create carousel posts around gifting and relatable Mother's Day topics.

How it will work

It allows us to show multiple ideas or products in one post and increases saves/shares.

Examples
5 Gifts She'll Actually Use This Mother's Day
Gift Guide for Every Mom
Track 03

Website + Social Combined Content

The emotional layer — the storytelling that makes the campaign feel intentional rather than sales-driven.

1

Founder's Note

What we would do

Share a short founder message around Mother's Day.

How it will work

It adds a personal and emotional layer to the campaign.

Example
A small note on what motherhood means and why this campaign matters.
2

Team Member Stories

What we would do

Ask team members to share what they got from their moms or what they would gift them.

How it will work

It makes the campaign feel more human and relatable.

Example
One thing I got from my mom is confidence / discipline / patience
3

Behind the Campaign Story

What we would do

Show the thought behind the Mother's Day campaign and connect it with the offer.

How it will work

It makes the campaign feel intentional, not just sales-driven.

Example
This Mother's Day, our offer is designed to make gifting easier and more meaningful
Summary

A Mother's Day campaign that combines website gifting experience, social media engagement, and emotional storytelling to drive both traffic and conversions.
