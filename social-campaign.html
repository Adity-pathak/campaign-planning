Why we're running this

Objective & core idea

Objective. Celebrate International Self-Care Day by getting people to talk about the simple habits that help them recharge — building engagement, growing followers, and deepening the emotional bond between Herbishh and the people who follow us.

Core idea. Everyone takes care of themselves differently. For some it's a relaxing hair spa; for others it's a book, a walk, or just enough sleep. We're asking one question — "what's one habit that feels like self-care to you?" — for a chance to win a Herbishh Self-Care Hair Care Kit.
