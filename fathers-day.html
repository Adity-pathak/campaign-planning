Sale Strategy Suggestion

Sale Strategy

Pride Month Shade Offer

Color shampoo and PPD-free range

How we can do it

Give a special offer on selected hair color shampoo shades or the PPD-free color shampoo range. Along with a generic sitewide offer, give a better discount on color shampoo and PPD-free range.

Why this is better

It feels more connected to the campaign instead of a random discount. Helps push the most campaign-relevant product category.
